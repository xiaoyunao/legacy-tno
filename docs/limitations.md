# Limits of the pilot

- The field selection searches at most one opposition-length window per selected field. Annual track extension remains unverified.
- The source photometry is integrated aperture flux with CP weight and gain. Near masks and on structured galaxies the model can be biased.
- The cross-night classification can retain persistent deep references close to their positions as ambiguous when the available span is short. Some true moving sources can lie near those references.
- Candidate growth uses a numerically evaluated linearized envelope and a finite tolerance. Completeness near bin edges, sparsely sampled seasons and outside the moving read footprint is not established.
- CCD quality and search budget exclusions remain visible in the run summaries. Areas are geometric coverage and do not imply completed search over all distances.
- DES-only matching leaves residual candidate identities unresolved. Image-level confirmation, a current all-object identity screen and absolute completeness require further work.
